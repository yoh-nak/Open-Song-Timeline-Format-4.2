## Provenance

Baseline inputs: OSTF 4.2.0 Publication Staging v3 and the OSTF 4.1.0 GitHub Publication archive supplied in this conversation. English/Japanese manuals are adapted from the 4.1 publication and the consolidated 4.2 text. The final 4.2 schema retains the identical `properties` and `$defs` projection; its release-status comment is editorially updated. The 4.1 base schema is preserved byte-for-byte.

Some technical fixtures are adapted from the 4.1 examples and 4.2 vocal examples. New supplemental field examples and three musical arrangements were created for this release. Historical software versions, legacy protocol identifiers and document provenance remain historical data rather than current release-status markers.
